Connection help & how feedback works

Use an HTTPS site or localhost. Enable wireless on your PM5 and disconnect ErgData or other apps using the same Bluetooth connection. Native Safari/Firefox do not support Web Bluetooth. iOS requires a Web Bluetooth browser; compatibility needs testing on your device.

The app reads Concept2 rowing status, additional status 1, and stroke data. Power is calculated from split using Concept2’s formula. Timing, length and work are measured by the PM5. No force curve or camera data is collected.

The score uses the last 12 complete strokes, with at least eight required. Pauses longer than 10 seconds clear the coaching window. Consistency compares variations in work (50%), total stroke time (30%) and drive length (20%). Rhythm compares recovery/drive time with your chosen target. These rules support practice; they are not a validated technique rating.

For interval workouts, finish and start a new recording for each piece. If the recorded stroke time or distance resets, the app saves the current recording automatically. Only actively received strokes are recorded; reconnect to continue after Bluetooth drops.
