Session settings

CONCEPT2 PM5 · ANDROID CHROME

Enable wireless on your PM5, then connect. Row with your phone in landscape. Swipe between the three screens or tap a tab.

HTTPS is required on your phone. Close ErgData if it holds the Bluetooth connection.

Targets are adjustable practice goals. Changing them finishes your current recording. The score uses 70% consistency and 30% rhythm. The 3D rower illustrates a reference stroke using monitor timing; it cannot reproduce or assess your body position.

Saved sessions

Latest 10 sessions stored on this device. Recording totals include received strokes only. Download JSON/CSV for retention. Demo data is explicitly labelled.

Connection & curve help

The coaching curve is the PM5’s force plot, requested after each drive using CSAFE. It is normalized to its peak because instantaneous handle velocity is not available to derive a power curve. If your firmware does not provide a curve, metrics and coaching continue.

The rower follows live stroke-state transitions, with drive/recovery durations from the most recent stroke. It stops extrapolating when telemetry stops. Reduced-motion settings use static phase poses.

Pause/resume affects recording; the monitor and coaching stay live. Bluetooth disconnect and counter resets save the current recording. The browser may suspend Bluetooth or revoke screen wake lock in the background.
